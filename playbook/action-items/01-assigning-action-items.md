## Overview

Action items capture follow-up work from a meeting—who is responsible, what is due, and current status.

## Steps

1. Open the meeting record after the session.
2. Go to the **Action items** area.
3. Click **Add action item** (or equivalent).
4. Enter a clear description, assign a member, and set a due date if applicable.
5. Save. Assignees receive notification when your app is configured to send them.

## Best practices

- Write action items as specific, measurable tasks.
- Review open items at the start of the next council or committee meeting.
- Close or reassign items when work is complete or deferred.
