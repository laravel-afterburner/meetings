## Purpose

Meeting notices inform {{ entity_label }} members about an upcoming session, including date, time, location, and how to participate.

## Steps

1. Open the meeting from **Meetings**.
2. Add or update notice content and any required attachments.
3. Use your organization's usual channel (email, portal announcement, or printed notice) to reach members.
4. Keep a copy in [Documents](/playbook/documents/overview) when you need a permanent record.

## Tips

- Send notices within the notice period required by your bylaws.
- For AGMs, link the final agenda and any circular resolutions before the meeting date.
