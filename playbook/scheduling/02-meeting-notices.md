## Purpose

Meeting notices inform {{ entity_label }} members about an upcoming session, including date, time, location, and how to participate.

## General workflow

1. Open the meeting from **Events → Meetings**.
2. Add or update notice content and any required attachments on the meeting record.
3. Distribute the notice through your organization's usual channels (email, portal announcement, or printed notice).
4. Keep a copy in [Documents](/help/documents/overview) when you need a permanent record.

## Strata applications

When your application integrates meeting notices with the Documents library, authorized council members can:

1. Open the meeting and use **Preview notice** to review the formatted notice before sending.
2. **Download** the notice as a PDF for email or printing.
3. **Save to Documents** to store the notice in your {{ entity_label }} library for AGM packages and records.

These actions require permission to manage meetings. Owners without that permission can still receive notices through other channels but cannot generate PDFs from the meeting page.

## Tips

- Send notices within the notice period required by your bylaws.
- For AGMs, link the final agenda and any circular resolutions before the meeting date.
- Pair notices with [Announcements](/help/communications/posting-announcements) when you want an in-app broadcast to all members.
