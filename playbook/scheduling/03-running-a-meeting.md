## Overview

After a meeting is scheduled, council runs the session from the **in-progress** workflow, records attendance and minutes, then marks the meeting complete.

## Starting the session

1. Open **Events → Meetings** and select the meeting.
2. Click **Start meeting** (or open **In progress**) when the session begins.
3. Record attendance and work through agenda items on the in-progress page.

## During the meeting

- Capture decisions and discussion notes as needed.
- Create or link [action items](/help/meetings/assigning-action-items) for follow-up work.
- Link [ballots](/help/voting/creating-a-ballot) when a formal vote is required.

## Minutes

On the meeting record or in-progress view:

1. Draft or edit **Meeting minutes** using the built-in editor.
2. Use **Generate draft** when your application provides a template with attendance and agenda sections.
3. **Save** progress during the meeting.
4. **Finalize** minutes when council approves the record. Finalized minutes become read-only for most users.

Strata applications may also offer **Download minutes** and **Save to Documents** for AGM packages — available to members with manage-meetings permission.

## Completing the meeting

When the session ends, mark the meeting **Complete**. The completed record shows finalized minutes, action items, and linked documents for future reference.
