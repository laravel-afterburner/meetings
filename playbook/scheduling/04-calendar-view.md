## Overview

The **Calendar** under **Events** shows upcoming and past meetings in a month view. Use it to spot scheduling conflicts and open meeting records quickly.

## Steps

1. Open **Events → Calendar** from the main navigation.
2. Browse months using the calendar controls.
3. Click a meeting entry to open its detail page.

## ICS subscription

When enabled, the calendar page provides a subscription URL you can add to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or other apps. Subscribed calendars refresh automatically so council members see updated dates without manual re-entry.

Share the subscription link only with members who should see meeting schedules.
